## 更新内容

### 新增

- **迷失之地战斗诊断日志**：战斗中每 2 秒在日志里输出一行「战斗诊断」，记录区域类型、脱战计数、画面识别结果（交互 / 距离 / 入口）、「前往下一个区域」提示、距离文字和阵亡计数。

用于排查「清完怪后角色还对着空气攻击、迟迟不退出战斗」的问题，该问题的根因还在确认中。遇到时请把日志里的「战斗诊断」行发到 Issue。

## What's Changed

### Added

- **Lost Void battle diagnostic log**: during a fight, one "战斗诊断" (battle diagnostic) line is written to the log every 2 seconds. It records the area type, the out-of-battle counter, what the screen recognition sees (interact / distance / entrance), the "go to next area" hint, the distance text and the death count.

It is meant for investigating the case where the character keeps attacking thin air after all enemies are cleared and the fight does not end. The root cause is still being confirmed. If you hit it, please attach the "战斗诊断" lines from the log to an Issue.
