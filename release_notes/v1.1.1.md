## 更新内容

### 修复

- **迷失之地寻路脱困**：此前只按 A/D 横移，遇到宽障碍或墙角会一直贴着障碍滑，脱不了困。现在加回后退和前进：第 2 次脱困起先后退再横移，绕过后前进，时长随本层卡住次数增加（后退最长 2 秒，前进最长 2 秒）。

---

## What's Changed

### Fixed

- **Lost Void stuck escape**: it used to only strafe with A/D, so against a wide obstacle or a corner the character kept sliding along it and never got free. Backing up and moving forward are back: from the 2nd escape attempt it backs up first, then strafes, then moves forward once clear. The durations grow with the number of times the character got stuck on the current floor (up to 2 seconds backward and up to 2 seconds forward).
